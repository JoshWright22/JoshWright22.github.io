Why a dollar

I want to learn to draw for real. That means years at a classical atelier, drawing from life every day. I want the best school I can get into.

I can't afford it yet. I didn't want to ask for donations, so I'm selling the thing school will teach me. Every commission is one more drawing of practice and one more dollar toward getting there.

A million is the dream. I'll draw as many as you want, but I only open as many each month as I can actually finish.

Questions

What can I commission?

Pretty much anything that fits in a sketch. People, pets, characters, creatures or a little scene. One subject per dollar.

What do they look like?

Quick ink sketches, like the ones on the Wall. Think sketchbook studies, not finished paintings. That's how I can do them for $1.

What if you don't finish mine?

If your commission isn't delivered within 30 days, you get your $1 back automatically. You don't have to ask.

What happens to my photo?

Only the drawing is ever shown. I delete your photo once your commission is sent.

Do I have to be on the Wall?

No. Use your name, a nickname, or stay anonymous. Say which in your order message.

Can I get more than one?

Yes. Each dollar is one commission.

AtOMoC SHeLiS

Placed #12 of 150 games. Project by Josh Wright.

I designed and built this chemistry themed roguelike auto battler solo, where you buy elements each round and connect them into a growing molecule that fights for you. I made it for the Club Club Jam Jam, which I also helped organize and run, and it placed #12. Since the jam I have been turning playtest feedback into a roadmap and expanding it into a Steam release covering all 118 elements.

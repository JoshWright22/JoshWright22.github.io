I'm Josh. I'm 24, I study computer science, and what I really want is to go to art school and learn to draw the classical way.

I didn't want to ask for donations, so I'm selling the thing school will teach me. Send a photo of you, a friend or your pet, and I'll draw the portrait by hand for a dollar. Every dollar goes to tuition.

You pay on Ko-fi and attach your photo. Portraits get drawn in order, and the full-size drawing comes to your inbox. Then it takes its frame on the wall above, so you can watch the wall fill up and watch me get better. I only open as many each month as I can finish.

What if you don't finish mine?

If your portrait isn't delivered within 30 days, you get your $1 back automatically. You don't have to ask.

What happens to my photo?

Only the drawing is ever shown. I delete your photo once your portrait is sent.

Do I have to be on the wall?

No. Use your name, a nickname, or stay anonymous. Say which in your order message.

Why I'm doing this

I want to learn to draw properly. The best training for that is a classical atelier, years of drawing from life. My goal is a full art education at the best school I can get into.

I can't pay for it yet. So instead of asking for donations, I'm selling the thing the school will teach me: portraits. Each $1 is one more drawing of practice and one more dollar toward tuition.

A million is the dream. I'll draw as many as people want, and only open as many spots each month as I can actually finish.

Questions

What if you don't finish mine?

If your portrait isn't delivered within 30 days of your order, your $1 is refunded automatically. You don't need to ask.

What happens to my photo?

Only the drawing is ever shown. Your photo is deleted once your portrait is sent.

Do I have to be on the Wall?

No. Use your name, a nickname, or stay anonymous. Say which in your order message.

What style are the portraits?

Quick ink head-and-shoulders portraits, like the ones on the Wall. They're studies, not finished paintings, which is why they cost $1.

Can I buy more than one?

Yes. Each $1 is one portrait.

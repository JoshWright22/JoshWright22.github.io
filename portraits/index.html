The method

I

The commission

Pay $1 through Ko-fi with PayPal or card. I never see your card details.

II

The sitter

Attach a photo to your order message. A face, a pet or a character all work.

III

The drawing

Portraits are drawn in order, one at a time. You'll know your place in line and the week to expect it.

IV

The portrait

The full-size drawing arrives in your inbox and joins the Wall, if you want it there.

ii.

The Wall

Every portrait hangs here in the order it was drawn. Portrait No. 1 and portrait No. 10,000 will share the same wall, so you can watch me get better.

Why a dollar

I didn't want to ask for donations, so I'm selling the thing school will teach me.

I want to learn to draw for real. That means years in a classical atelier, drawing from life every day, at the best school I can get into.

I can't afford it yet. Every portrait is one more drawing of practice and one more dollar toward getting there.

A million is the dream. I'll draw as many as you want, but I only open as many each month as I can actually finish.

iv.

Questions

What do the portraits look like?

Hand-drawn head and shoulders studies, like the ones on the Wall. Think sketchbook studies, not finished paintings. That's how I can do them for $1.

What if you don't finish mine?

If your portrait isn't delivered within 30 days, you get your $1 back automatically. You don't have to ask.

What happens to my photo?

Only the drawing is ever shown. I delete your photo once your portrait is sent.

Do I have to be on the Wall?

No. Use your name, a nickname, or stay anonymous. Say which in your order message.

Can I order more than one?

Yes. Each dollar is one portrait.
